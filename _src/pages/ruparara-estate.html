A blank canvas.
A developer's dream.

This 1,300-hectare canvas lies in scenic Nyanga. It has breathtaking scenery, undulating terrain with panoramic views, clean air and room to imagine almost anything.

Natural water features suit aquaculture and irrigation, the rich soils suit high-value crops, and the land sits close to the region's tourism corridors and sites.
